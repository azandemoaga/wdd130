White Water Rafting

Experience the raw power of the river like never before. Whether you are looking for heart-pounding Class IV rapids or a scenic float through breathtaking canyons, our expert guides are here to lead the way. Join us for a journey you will remember for a lifetime!

History

Founded in 2024, Rafting Adventure Club started with just two rafts and a passion for the Salmon River. Over the last few decades, we have expanded to three locations and helped over 50,000 adventurers navigate the most beautiful rapids in the country.

Our commitment to safety and fun has never wavered. We continue to innovate in equipment and guide training every year, ensuring that every guest leaves with a smile and a story they will tell for a lifetime.
