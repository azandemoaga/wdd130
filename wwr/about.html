Rafting Adventure Club

Our mission is to provide life-changing outdoor experiences. We believe that the rush of the river brings people together and creates memories that last a lifetime. Whether you are a beginner or an expert, we have a paddle waiting for you.

History

Founded in 2024, Rafting Adventure Club started with just two rafts and a passion for the Salmon River. Over the last few decades, we have expanded to three locations and helped over 50,000 adventurers navigate the most beautiful rapids in the country. Our commitment to safety and fun has never wavered.
